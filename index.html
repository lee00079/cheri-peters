About the Speaker

Cheri Peters

Cheri Peters is an international speaker, author and trauma recovery advocate. Through her television ministry Celebrating Life in Recovery, featured on Hope Channel, Firstlight and 3ABN, she has helped thousands discover hope after trauma, abuse and loss. Her compassionate, faith-filled presentations combine biblical truth with practical insight into emotional healing.
